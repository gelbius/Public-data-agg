        + Method

        Every number on this page comes from a public government series.

        A scheduled job downloads each series from FRED (Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis) and the BLS Consumer Expenditure Survey. A build step then computes every figure and headline, and the site redeploys. No API keys and no private data.

        • Hero phase labels (e.g. "Energy shock") are our interpretation of the data, dated from Fed rate moves and inflation turning points. They are not official designations.
        • BLS did not publish October 2025 CPI or unemployment figures. Lines bridge that gap.
        • The revolving credit series shows a sharp one-month drop in December 2024. The chart flags it rather than smoothing it away.
        • Household spending (Consumer Expenditure Survey) is annual and lags by about nine months.
        • Recession shading uses the NBER-based indicator (USREC).
